What We Do

A trusted broker between the oilfield and the AI frontier.

We license Texas oil & gas companies' anonymized operational data to the labs training the world's most capable AI models.

Industrial data is AI's next frontier.

Frontier models have largely exhausted the public internet. What they need now is specialized, high-fidelity data from the physical world — the kind that lives on servers in Midland, Houston, and Fort Worth.

Texas operators have spent decades capturing exactly that: drilling parameters, completion designs, SCADA and sensor telemetry, maintenance histories, production curves, and field reports. Most of it sits idle after its operational use is done.

Lonestar Data identifies what's valuable, strips it of anything sensitive or identifying, packages it to lab specifications, and negotiates licenses that put real dollars back on your balance sheet.
