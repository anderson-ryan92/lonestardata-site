How It Works

Five steps from server room to revenue.

A disciplined, confidential process built for operators who take data security seriously.

  1. Confidential Discovery

    Under NDA, we review the types, volume, and history of the operational data you hold — no data leaves your hands at this stage.

  2. Valuation

    We assess market demand and give you a realistic range for what your datasets could earn from frontier AI labs.

  3. Anonymization & Packaging

    Identifiers, locations, and sensitive commercial details are removed. Data is cleaned, documented, and structured to lab specifications.

  4. Licensing & Negotiation

    We bring your data to qualified buyers and negotiate the license — price, scope, exclusivity, and usage restrictions — on your behalf.

  5. Delivery & Payment

    Data is delivered securely, payment flows to you, and we manage ongoing renewals and new license opportunities.

★

You own your data. Always.

We never buy or take title to your data. Every deal is a license you approve — you decide what's shared, with whom, and for how long. You can decline any deal at any stage.
